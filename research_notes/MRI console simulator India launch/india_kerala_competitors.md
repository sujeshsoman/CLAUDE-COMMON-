## Q1. Who in Kerala or India has recently launched a virtual MRI console simulator, MRI simulation lab or "hands-on MRI console training"?

### Takeaway
I could not find a confirmed Kerala-based virtual MRI *console* simulator launch. The closest Kerala match is **SIMLABS (Thrissur)**, a medical-simulation training company whose site says it launched in 2026 and lists "Radiology Simulation" (patient positioning, protocol selection), but no MRI console product. The clearest **Indian** browser-based MRI console simulator is **ScanFlo** (Zeka Labs Pvt Ltd, Delhi). It covers MRI, CT, PET-CT and X-ray, prices in INR, and is resold by a Delhi/Noida paramedical institute. Neither is clearly the "someone in Kerala" the user heard about, so the user should ask their source for the name.

## Q2. Which Indian institutes, hospitals and academies sell MRI technologist training, MRI fellowships or advanced MRI training for radiographers, at what price and for how long? Do any use simulators?

### Takeaway
In Kerala and South India, MRI is taught mainly inside 3 to 4-year BSc MRT/MIT/B.MRIT degrees and 1-year certificate or diploma courses. I found **no stand-alone, published-price "advanced MRI console" course for working radiographers in Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, Kozhikode, Bengaluru, Chennai or Mangaluru**. Vendor programmes (Siemens Healthineers India MR Specialization, GE HealthCare India clinical education, Philips × Star Imaging MRI School) are the main structured MRI upskilling routes. Siemens is the only one confirmed to use a simulator. Many Kerala radiographers spend on Gulf licensing-exam coaching (DHA/MOH/DOH) rather than MRI skills courses.

### Cited Findings
**Kerala**
- Amrita (Kochi) BSc Medical Radiology and Imaging Technology. The Kochi listing cites GE Signa HDxt 1.5T and GE Discovery MR750W scanners for training — [Amrita B.MRIT](https://www.amrita.edu/program/bachelor-medical-radiology-imaging-technology/)

### Inferences
- There is a visible gap in Kerala: no affordable, MRI-specific skills product for working radiographers or final-year students between a degree and a first MRI job, and none in Malayalam. - With pay around ₹18K to ₹32K a month in private Kerala jobs, a sub-₹1,000/month or one-off ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 price is likely the realistic range for individuals, consistent with ScanFlo's ₹899 to ₹999 entry point. This is an inference, not a measured willingness to pay.
- Gulf-exam coaching (AEON, GCC Academy) is an adjacent spend category and a possible partnership or bundling channel: "MRI console practice plus DHA/MOH prep".

- Apart from Siemens, I could not confirm whether any Indian institute uses Corsmed, ScanFlo or another simulator in its curriculum (the 3D Paramedical/ScanFlo resale aside).
